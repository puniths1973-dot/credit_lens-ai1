# Credit Lens AI 🚀
### Alternative Credit Scoring & Financial Behavior Evaluation Engine

**Credit Lens AI** is an advanced alternative credit scoring platform designed to replace legacy credit bureaus (like CIBIL). It evaluates real-time cash flow, digital wallet inflows, utility payments, and spending habits to assess the true repayment capacity of students, freelancers, gig workers, and first-time borrowers who lack traditional credit histories.

## 🔥 Key Features
1. **Dynamic Financial Health Scoring (FHS):** Computes a real-time risk score based on income stability, cash flow volatility, and liquidity buffers.
2. **Self-Reported Simulation Mode:** Allows users and judges to dynamically adjust income, gig earnings, rent, and monthly liabilities to watch the repayment capacity update instantly.
3. **CIBIL Blindspot Detector (Comparison Matrix):** Side-by-side analytical breakdown proving how legacy credit bureaus reject thin-file earners while Credit Lens AI accurately assesses them.
4. **Interactive Chart.js Visualizations:** Real-time visual tracking of cash flow trajectories and categorical expense breakdowns.
5. **Institutional Underwriter Portal:** Provides lenders with automated risk tiers, AI bank statement verification features, and transparent data attribution.
